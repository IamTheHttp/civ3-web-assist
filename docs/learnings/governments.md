# Government what-if

- "Try government" and the Governments panel recompute each city's tiles under another government, then corruption and
  waste (the game's rule at that corruption level) and building upkeep, anchored to the game's own figures.
- Maya turn 194 (80% science): leaving Republic loses the trade bonus, 345 of 895 commerce. Communism keeps 390 net
  commerce but 371 net shields (Republic 452 / 269). Despotism's tile penalty cuts net food from +103 to +28.
- Anarchy wastes every coin and shield: from the exe, and the player confirmed it in-game.
- Communism (communal) corruption is from the exe, not checked against a save.
- Not modelled: unit support, war weariness, happiness and martial law, hurrying.
