Civ3 Empire Viewer

Open a save to see your cities

Drop a .SAV file here, or use Open save…

Autosaves are in your Conquests folder under Saves\Auto. Use Watch autosave folder… (Edge or Chrome) to refresh every turn.

For building and unit names and the Courthouse what-if, also use Load rules (conquests.biq)… once: it's in your Conquests folder.

Nothing is uploaded: the files are read inside this page.

Grow: turns until the city grows (same rule as the city screen); – not growing, max food box full and waiting for an Aqueduct or Hospital. Food: net food per turn. Box: food stored / needed. Shields and Commerce: raw totals from the city's tiles, before waste and corruption. Net Shields and Net Commerce: shields after waste and commerce after corruption. Waste and Corrupt: shields and commerce lost, and as a share of the city's total (%; the totals row shows your whole empire). Resist: resisting citizens. Foreign: citizens of another nationality (culture-flip risk). Spec: entertainers / scientists / taxmen. CH / PS: the city has a Courthouse / Police Station. Tick or untick to see what that would change: corruption, waste and their shares show now → then. A what-if tick (something the city doesn't really have, or a building you've unticked) is outlined in amber; plain ticks are what the city has. CH Effect / PS Effect: what building a Courthouse / Police Station would gain the city each turn: commerce kept, after its Library, Marketplace etc. (com), shields kept (shd) and the building's gold upkeep (upk). The two buildings reduce corruption the same way; the Police Station costs more upkeep. Sorting by them ranks the best places, upkeep included. Lib, Univ, Lab, Mkt, Bank, SE (and their Effect columns) work the same way for buildings that boost science or gold: the effect is what the building would add to this city, worked out the way the game does it (your slider split, then the building bonuses), minus upkeep. Ticking a building also ticks what it requires. Use View to switch between column sets and Columns… to choose what a view shows. Corrupt % over the cap shows the uncapped parts in brackets: distance to the palace + city rank (hover for details). Corruption figures come from the game's formula and are checked against your save (see Diagnostics); ~ marks a city where they didn't match. Click a column header to sort; click again to reverse.
